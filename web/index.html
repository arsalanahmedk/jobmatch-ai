          How scoring works

          JobMatch AI is transparent on purpose. Skills are detected from a curated taxonomy with alias and word-boundary aware matching — so “Java” never matches “JavaScript”. The score is 65% required-skill coverage + 35% keyword overlap. Everything runs locally on this server; no API keys, no accounts, and your pasted text is never stored.
